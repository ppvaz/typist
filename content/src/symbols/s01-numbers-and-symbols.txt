In the workshop, measurements follow a simple rule: cut once, measure twice, and
write every number down. A shelf 1200 mm long needs 3 brackets spaced 450 mm
apart; a 900 mm shelf needs only 2. The glue sets in 20-30 minutes at 18 C, but
it needs 24 hours before any load over 15 kg. Keep the offcuts under 40 cm in the
blue bin (not the red one!) and label each bin with its date: 03/09, 10/09, 17/09.
A recipe for 4 people uses 500 g of flour, 350 ml of water, 10 g of salt, and 7 g
of yeast. For 6 people multiply by 1.5: that is 750 g, 525 ml, 15 g, and 10.5 g.
Bake at 230 degrees for 35-40 minutes; the loaf is done when its base sounds
hollow. Score = (crust * 2 + crumb) / 3, rounded to 1 decimal place.
