01

Direction and rationale

A quiet instrument, not a scoreboard

Typist is used for thirty minutes at a stretch by one person who is deliberately slowing down. The surface is warm paper rather than white glare, structure comes from hairline rules instead of boxes and shadows, and nothing moves unless the person moved it. Strong emphasis is rationed to three things: the active mode, the target text, and the next action.

Keycap geometry as the grid

Everything is built on a 4 px rhythm and the 48 px key unit of a US ANSI board, so panels, rows and the keyboard map share one measure. Surfaces carry a 2 px bottom ridge — the keycap's own edge — which is the single tactile motif in the system. Radii stay at 4–7 px: made, not soft.

Colour means hand, never mood

Two accents at one lightness and chroma, differing only in hue, carry the only identity that matters: slate for the left hand, ochre for the right. Status uses a third family and never appears without a word or a shape beside it, because a missed gate must read at a glance and in monochrome.

Typefaces
Newsreader Editorial voice. Headings and every large metric — a serif numeral reads as a measurement, not a high score.
IBM Plex Sans Interface prose. Drawn for technical work, plain at 12–14 px, never chatty.
JetBrains Mono The practice face, plus every code, key name and tabular figure. Fixed advance width keeps a character from shifting position when the one before it is wrong — the strict positional model made visible. Disambiguated l/1/I and a slashed zero.
Principles
01Accuracy is stated before speed, everywhere, and speed can be switched off.
02A number with no evidence behind it says No evidence, not zero.
03Compact rows and quiet dividers for information; generous air for the typing surface.
04A missed day is a plan to adjust, never a debt or a broken streak.
05The app reports what it observed and asks about what it cannot see.

Accessibility contract
01Every control is reachable and operable by keyboard, including mode selection, recovery and logging. Focus is visible at 2 px and never suppressed; focus is never trapped in the typing area.
02The typing surface is a real textarea with an accessible label. Results are announced once, on completion — never per keystroke.
03No state is carried by colour alone: filled square = met, rotated outline = missed, hollow circle = pending, dashed = no evidence, and each is accompanied by a word.
04Body text, labels and captions hold 4.5:1 or better on their own ground in both themes; headline metrics hold at least 3:1. The one deliberate exception is --ink-upcoming at ≈3.8:1, used only for not-yet-typed characters at 30 px. Type size is adjustable and the practice face may be enlarged independently.
05Shortcuts are optional and remappable, because a comfortable hand position changes with the mode. Tab always leaves, Esc always ends, and OS layout-switch shortcuts are never consumed.
06Reduced motion removes every transition in the table in 02; the caret becomes solid and skeletons become static.
Writing rules for product copy
Say what happened and what would come next, in one sentence. “Accuracy reached the target. Two more qualifying sessions are needed.” Name the missing condition, never a verdict on the person. “Speed was 1.1 WPM short of 20 on two of three trials.” Mark inference as inference: may be, looks like, candidate. Never state which finger or hand was used — the app cannot know. Missing data is “No evidence”, “Unknown” or “Not measured”. Never 0, never “—” alone, never a blank cell. Numbers keep their denominator or sample size. Physical positions are written in mono as codes: KeyY, not “the Y key”. No streaks, no congratulation, no penalty for stopping. Rest is a legitimate outcome of a logged session.
